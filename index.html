About Us

Team Nanda’s is a dynamic group of young artists united by their deep-rooted love for Uttarakhand’s folk music and culture. With the goal of preserving and promoting the musical heritage of the Devbhoomi, the team beautifully blends traditional Uttarakhandi rhythms with contemporary instrumentation — creating a fresh yet soulful sound. Every performance reflects the melodies of the mountains, the traditions of the land, and the spirit of the people who call it home. Through music, Team Nanda’s keeps the essence of Uttarakhand alive, while making it connect with today’s generation.

Our Legacy — From One Generation to the Next



The First Generation

The founding members of Team Nanda’s laid a strong foundation for the band’s mission and identity. With passion and purpose, they began their journey to revive and reinterpret the rich folk traditions of Uttarakhand, blending them with the energy and creativity of contemporary music. Their dedication to preserving the soul of traditional rhythms while experimenting with modern sounds created a distinctive style that continues to define the band today. This pioneering group not only shaped the band’s artistic vision but also inspired a community of young musicians to take pride in their cultural roots. Through countless rehearsals, campus performances, and cultural festivals, they transformed simple beginnings into a recognised musical movement.

The Second Generation

The Second Generation of Team Nanda’s carried forward the legacy with renewed enthusiasm and fresh creative energy. Building upon the strong foundation laid by their seniors, they embraced the responsibility of preserving the band’s essence while introducing new ideas and sounds. This group of passionate artists brought modern perspectives to traditional melodies — blending innovative arrangements, harmonies, and stage presence to reach wider audiences.

Team Members:

Anoop Bharti, Neha Painuly, Ayush Thapliyal, Shiva, Taneesha Rawat, Ayush Jadli, Bhawna Bisht



The Third Generation

Fuelled by creativity and collaboration, this generation redefined the band’s identity — introducing new genres, fusion elements, and dynamic performances that showcased both technical finesse and emotional depth. They took Team Nanda’s spirit beyond boundaries, connecting with listeners across regions through online platforms, live concerts, and original compositions.

Team Members:

Uttam , Priyanshi Gusain, Kuldeep, Anukriti



The Fourth Generation

The Fourth Generation of Team Nanda’s marks a new wave of enthusiasm, creativity, and promise. As the freshest batch of students, they step into the spotlight with the same spirit of passion and unity that has defined the band since its beginning. Eager to learn, perform, and carry the name forward, this generation brings a youthful spark — blending curiosity with dedication to keep the rhythm of Team Nanda’s alive.

Team Members:

Priyanshi thapliyal, Anjali, Deekshit, Nitaye, Amritanshu, Sarthak, Ayushsharma, Abhishek.
